## What this means in practice

- **28 equivalences** (22%) have every gate mapped to an existing LeanQuantum matrix with a numerically-verified, phase-correct convention. These are genuinely ready for someone to sit down and try to close the `sorry`. Several of them (the `h` gate's U-gate and `u2` decompositions) restate lemmas LeanQuantum already has, just from the opposite direction, which is a promising sign the two matrix conventions were built compatibly by coincidence.
- **16 equivalences** need exactly one small helper lemma first (mainly the `rz = exp(-iθ/2) • phaseShift(θ)` relationship, and the `sdg`/`tdg` inverse-gate relationships) before they become 'direct'-tier.
- **23 equivalences** are blocked on the cx/cnot-vs-notc qubit-ordering question — see `notes/qubit_ordering.md`. This single open question gates a disproportionate number of two-qubit equivalences, so it's the highest-leverage thing to resolve first.
- **63 equivalences** need genuinely new LeanQuantum definitions (multi-controlled gates, the Ising-coupling family rxx/ryy/rzz/rzx, hardware-native gates like ecr). These are real contributions to LeanQuantum itself, not just bridge work.
